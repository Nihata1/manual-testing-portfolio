# TC-CART-001 — Add a Product to the Cart

## Preconditions

- The user is signed in to SauceDemo as `standard_user`.
- The Products page is open and the cart is empty.

## Test Data

- **Product** — Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt

## Test Steps

1. Click **Add to cart** for the selected product.
2. Check the item count on the cart icon.
3. Open the cart and check its contents.

## Expected Result

- The cart icon shows `1`.
- The cart lists **Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt** with a quantity of `1`.
